About

A hackathon run like a product team, not a party

CodeStorm is the flagship build weekend of IIM Lucknow's Noida campus, now in its fourth edition. Three hundred–odd teams apply, we take the strongest, and for 36 hours the campus turns into one very caffeinated engineering floor.

What makes it different is the brief. Every problem statement comes from an operating company with a real constraint attached — a reconciliation backlog, a thin-file borrower, four hundred suppliers with no emissions data. You are not building a demo for a slide. You are building the first version of something a team could actually run on Monday.

The jury is faculty, engineering leaders and investors, and the rubric is published before the event rather than after it. Teams of exactly three, because that is the smallest group that still has to communicate.

🎓 Open to every campus

Students from any recognised Indian institution, plus 2025–26 graduates. Last edition brought teams from 64 colleges.

🧑‍🏫 Forty mentors on the floor

Two structured mentoring rounds with practitioners from the sponsor engineering teams, in 15-minute blocks you book yourself.

🚀 A path after the weekend

Finalists get office hours at the IIML Enterprise Incubation Centre, and the title sponsor fast-tracks winners through its interview loop.

    ① Eligibility

    • ✓Enrolled in a recognised Indian undergraduate, postgraduate or diploma programme at the time of the event, or graduated on or after 1 June 2025.
    • ✓Aged 18 or over on 14 November 2026. Photo ID and a college ID are checked at the gate.
    • ✓Open to every campus in India. You do not need any affiliation with IIM Lucknow.
    • ✓One team per person. Registering on two teams voids both.
    • ✗Full-time employees of the organising committee, the jury, or any sponsor's hackathon team cannot compete.
    • ✗Teams that have already won a cash prize with the same project at another hackathon in 2026.

    ② Team composition

    ⚠

    Exactly three members. No exceptions. Not two, not four. The registration form will not issue a team ID until all three are filled in, and a team that arrives with two members cannot check in.

    • ✓Members may come from different colleges, different years and different disciplines. Mixed teams tend to score better on Design and Pitch.
    • ✓One member is designated Team Lead at registration. All official email goes to them and they speak for the team in disputes.
    • ✓Substitutions are allowed up to 7 November by writing to the organisers. After that the roster is frozen.
    • ✓No member may be replaced mid-event, and a team that drops to two members may keep building but forfeits the podium prizes.

    ③ Code of conduct

    CodeStorm follows a simple standard: everyone leaves the weekend wanting to come back. The organising committee may remove any participant, without refund or appeal, for a breach of the following.

    • ✓Respect. No harassment, discrimination or intimidation on any basis — including but not limited to gender, caste, religion, disability, orientation or institution.
    • ✓Honesty. Your submission must be your team's own work, built in the window. Declare every AI assistant, library and template you used.
    • ✓Safety. No attacking the event infrastructure, other teams' machines, or sponsor systems beyond the sandboxes you are given.
    • ✓Care. Sleep, eat, and look after the people next to you. The quiet room is open all night and mentors are not a scoring mechanism.

    Report a problem to any organiser in a purple lanyard, or confidentially to codestorm@iiml.example. Reports are handled by two committee members and never by the person reported.

    ④ Submission format

    Submit through the Submit tab. You may edit as often as you like until the deadline; the last saved version is the one judged.

    ⑤ Judging criteria

    Every project is scored out of 100 by at least three judges; the top ten by mean score go to the finals, where the full jury re-scores the pitch.
