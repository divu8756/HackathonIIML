Instructions & rules

How the hackathon works

Read these before you register. Breaking a core rule leads to disqualification — the judges' decision is final.

1

Teams of exactly three

Every team must have three members — no more, no less. Solo or duo entries are merged into teams at check-in.

2

Who can participate

Open to students of IIM Lucknow and invited partner colleges. Carry a valid college ID to the venue.

3

Build during the event

Code must be written within the 36-hour window. Bringing pre-built projects or reusing prior submissions is not allowed.

4

Any stack, all open source

Use any language, framework or public API. Cite everything you didn't write. Paid APIs are allowed if you cover the cost.

5

Submit on time

Push your repo and a 3-minute demo video before the Sunday 12:00 deadline. Late commits are ignored by judges.

6

Judging criteria

Business impact 30% · Technical execution 30% · Innovation 20% · Design & UX 10% · Pitch 10%.

Free

Registration is free. Meals, Wi-Fi and workspace for all 36 hours are provided on campus. Bring your own laptop and charger.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does my team really need three members? +

Yes. Teams must have exactly three members. If you register solo or as a pair, we'll help you find teammates at check-in so every team fields three.

Is there a registration fee? +

No. CodeStorm '26 is completely free to enter, and food and workspace are provided throughout the 36 hours.

How is the ₹10,00,000 split? +

Winner ₹5,00,000, runner-up ₹3,00,000, second runner-up ₹1,50,000, and a ₹50,000 Best Track award — ₹10,00,000 in total. Each team's prize is shared equally among its three members.

Do I need to be a coder? +

Not entirely. The strongest teams mix product, business and engineering. As long as your team can build and pitch a working prototype, you're set.

Can I use AI tools and open-source libraries? +

Absolutely — use any open-source library, public API or AI assistant. Just credit what you didn't build yourself in your submission.
